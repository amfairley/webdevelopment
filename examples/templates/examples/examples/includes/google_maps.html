To add an interactive section of Google Maps to your website, first go to Google Maps to the location that you want to show (at the desired zoom level), then right click and select "share this location". Click on the "Embed a map" option and it will provide you the HTML. Simply copy the provided <iframe></iframe> element into your HTML file. It will come with pre-set height and width dimensions but you can target it with your own custom CSS rules.
