Here, the text goes over the whole background. In this example on larger screens the text will only cover the left hand side of the image, with a second, empty column covering the right hand side. On smaller screens the text covers the whole image (col-12) and the right hand column is removed (d-none). On larger screens the right hand column appears by turning the display on (d-lg-block) and setting it to half the horizontal space (col-6). Of course this can be easily altered to just have the left hand column be col-12 the whole time and remove the right hand column to keep the text covering the whole image on any screen size.

The text has a dark background with an opacity of 0.2 so that the lighter coloured font can be read whilst still being able to see the background image. This is done as any light areas of the background the image would obscure light coloured text (or dark areas would obscure dark coloured text).
